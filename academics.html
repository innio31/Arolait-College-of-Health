Our Academic Programs

Apply Now
01

Public Health

Public Health is the science and practice of promoting and protecting the health and well-being of populations through education, research, policy-making, and community-based efforts. Unlike clinical healthcare, which focuses on treating individuals, public health aims to prevent diseases, prolong life, and improve the quality of life for entire communities.

Public health professionals work on a wide range of issues, including disease prevention, health education, epidemiology, environmental health, and health policy.

Learn More
02

Medical Laboratory Science

Medical Laboratory Science prepares students to perform complex diagnostic tests that help detect, diagnose, and treat diseases. Our state-of-the-art labs and experienced faculty ensure hands-on training in hematology, microbiology, clinical chemistry, and immunology.

Graduates are equipped to work in hospitals, research labs, and public health facilities, playing a vital role in patient care.

Learn More
03

Health Information Management

Health Information Management combines healthcare, technology, and business administration. Students learn to manage patient data, medical records, and health information systems while ensuring compliance with legal and ethical standards.

This program leads to careers in hospitals, insurance companies, and healthcare consulting.

Learn More
04

Community Health Extension

Community Health Extension focuses on delivering primary healthcare services to rural and underserved communities. Students gain skills in health promotion, disease prevention, maternal and child health, and basic clinical procedures.

Graduates become frontline healthcare providers and community change-makers.

Learn More
05

Dental Health Technology

Dental Health Technology trains students in oral hygiene, dental assisting, and preventive dental care. The curriculum includes both theoretical knowledge and clinical practice in modern dental facilities.

Graduates are prepared to work in dental clinics, hospitals, and public health programs.

Learn More
06

Pharmacy Technology

Pharmacy Technology provides students with the skills needed to assist pharmacists in dispensing medications, managing inventory, and providing patient education. The program emphasizes accuracy, ethics, and pharmaceutical best practices.

Graduates can work in retail pharmacies, hospital pharmacies, and pharmaceutical companies.

Learn More

Arolait Global College of Health Technology, approved by NBTE, Ministry of Education, offers comprehensive health technology programs.
